## Before you send anything

1. **Check each name against Nottoway's own list.** These are brokers who list
   trades and service companies at $1M to $5M in Virginia, the Carolinas, New
   York, New Jersey and Pennsylvania. That is Nottoway's buy box. Several of
   them (1, 4 and 6 especially) have listings today that Nottoway might want
   to buy. For each broker, decide which relationship comes first: buyer, or
   someone with a product. If you are already talking to one of them about a
   deal, skip them here. This touches Nottoway's broker relationships, so it
   is worth a word with Eric first.
2. **Reopen the evidence link before you quote a listing.** Listings were read
   on 2026-11-10 and move fast. I spot-checked three entries myself (2, 3 and
   8) and the contact details matched, except that the Horizon contact page
   shows only the general address; the direct address came from their team
   page, which I did not reopen.
3. **Read the opening lines as drafts.** A few lean on personal history from
   the broker's own bio. Keep that only if it reads as respectful to you.
4. **Never guess an email address.** Where none is published, use the contact
   page or the phone.

## Pace

- Day one: orders 1 to 4, from an address with history. See `START-HERE.md`
  for why not the new domain on its first day.
- Day three or later: orders 5 to 12, a few a day, after reading the first
  replies and fixing the pitch.
- One follow-up each, four or five business days later. Then stop.
- Keep a do-not-contact note for anyone who asks.

## What the list is weak on

- Only 9 of the 12 have a published email. The rest are phone or contact form.
- For 5 of the 12 the named person may not be the owner (5, 6, 8, 9, 12).
- Almost none of these brokers has written about AI. The signals are about
  screening, NDAs and proof of funds. Lead with that, not with agents.
- Nothing in West Virginia or DC, and Maryland is thin.
