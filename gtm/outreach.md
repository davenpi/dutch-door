# Outreach kit

Drafts for ID to edit and send. Nothing here has been sent to anyone.
Drafted by Claude for ID, 2026-11-10.

The list of who to write to is in `first-dozen.md`. Each entry there has a
one-line opening written for that person. Put it where the email says
`[their line]`.

## What you are asking for

Fifteen minutes and an honest reaction. Not a sale. The offer is to set up one
of their listings by hand at no charge. What you want to learn is in the
scorecard at the bottom.

## First email

Subject: Buyer inquiries on your listings

Hi [first name],

I'm Ian Davenport. My partner and I buy small service and construction
businesses, so I spend a lot of time on the buyer's side of a broker's inbox:
sending inquiries, signing NDAs, waiting for the package.

[their line]

I've built something for your side of that exchange and I'd like your honest
reaction to it. It is a front door for a listing. It answers a buyer's first
questions from your blind teaser, collects the NDA and proof of funds under
your rules, and only then hands the buyer to you with a short summary. It also
does a blind price check: the buyer enters the most they would pay, you enter
the least your seller would take, and each side learns only whether a deal is
possible.

I'm setting up the first dozen brokers by hand, at no charge. Could I have 15
minutes this week or next to show you, and to hear where it would break in
your practice?

[site address]

Ian Davenport
[phone]
[postal address]
If you would rather not hear from me again, reply and say so.

Notes on this email:

- The last two lines are there for a reason. US law (CAN-SPAM) covers
  one-to-one business email too. It asks for an honest subject, a real postal
  address (a street address, a PO box or a registered private mailbox all
  count) and a way to opt out that you honor. See
  `research/names-and-setup.md`, section 3. Not legal advice.
- On day one, send from an address with history and consider leaving the site
  link out, offering to send it in your reply. A domain registered that
  morning sits on a spam blocklist for its first 24 hours.
- No attachments, no tracking pixels, no link shorteners, no mail-merge tool.
  Write each one.

## If they say yes: the 15 minutes

Ask first, show second. The questions matter more than the demo.

1. On a typical listing, about how many inquiries come in, and how many of
   those people sign the NDA?
2. Walk me through what you do with a new inquiry today. What tools touch it?
3. How do you check that a buyer can fund the deal? At what point?
4. What is the most annoying part of that process?
5. (Show the listing page and the summary a broker gets. Three minutes.)
6. What would have to be true for you to put one listing behind this?
7. (Explain the blind price check in two sentences.) Would you ever enter a
   seller's floor into a tool like this? Why or why not?
8. If this worked, what would it be worth to you? How do you pay for tools
   today: per month, per listing, per deal?
9. Who else should I talk to?

Write the answers down the same day, in their words.

## Things to be straight about

- **You are also a buyer.** Say it in the first line, as above. If you are in
  an active conversation with a broker about a deal for Nottoway, think twice
  before pitching them a product in the same week. Both asks get weaker.
- **It is a pilot.** A person checks each buyer's paperwork for now. Booking
  does not sync with a calendar yet. Say so if asked.
- **The price check rests on trusting the operator.** If a broker asks how
  private it is, the honest answer is in `arbiter/README.md`.
- **Do not promise a price.** "Free during the pilot" is the only commitment.

## Scorecard: what the first dozen should tell you

Fill this in after each call. It is how you decide whether to keep going.

| Question | Good sign | Bad sign |
|---|---|---|
| Is buyer screening a real cost? | They name hours per week or a person who does it | "My assistant handles it, it's fine" |
| Would they put one listing behind it? | "Send me what you need" | "Interesting, keep me posted" |
| Would they enter a seller's floor? | Yes, with conditions | "Never", from most of them |
| Will they pay? | They name a number or a comparison | Free is the only interest |
| Do they bring up AI agents on their own? | Buyers' bots are already hitting their inbox | Blank look |
