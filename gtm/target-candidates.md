# First outreach candidates (business brokers)

Researched 2026-11-10. Research only. Nobody was contacted and no form was submitted.

## Method note

- Goal: about 20 US business brokers or small M&A firms that sell local service, trades and construction businesses at roughly $1M to $5M, where the named person can say yes to a pilot.
- How candidates were found: web searches by state and trade, then the firm's own listings, team and contact pages. Where a firm site would not open, I used an official directory or profile page (IBBA state directory, the Carolinas-Virginia Business Broker Association, BizBuySell broker profiles).
- Every candidate below was confirmed on at least one page I opened. Each section lists those pages.
- Contact details are only those the firm or an association publishes for business use. No email address was guessed. Where no email is published, the contact page is given.
- Listing counts, prices and statuses are as shown on 2026-11-10. Listings move fast. Re-open the evidence link before you quote a listing in an email.
- Limits you should know about:
  - BizBuySell and BizQuest profile pages mostly did not show listings to my fetch tool, so "listing count" comes from firm sites where possible.
  - Several firm sites could not be opened at all (listed at the end). Firms I could only see through a directory are marked as thin.
  - I found very little public writing by these brokers about AI. Bonus signals are almost all about buyer screening, NDAs and proof of funds.
- Ranking: fit to the $1M to $5M trades range first, then decision-maker clarity, number of active listings, bonus signals, and region.

Region count: 18 of 20 are in the weighted region (VA 5, NC 3, NY 3, PA 3, NJ 3, MD 1). Two are elsewhere.
