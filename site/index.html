The problem

Every listing draws a pile of inquiries. Few of them are buyers.

Most never sign the NDA. Some sign it and cannot fund the deal. The serious buyer waits in the same queue as everyone else, and you find out who is who one phone call at a time.

The questions are the same on every listing, and you answer them again for each new name.

  • "Is this still available?"Never signs the NDA
  • "Send me the financials."Never signs the NDA
  • "What's the address?"Wants details first
  • "NDA signed. Lender letter attached."The buyer you want, waiting in line
  • "Just looking around."Cannot fund the deal

How it works

Three steps before your phone rings

  1. 1

    Ask

    A buyer asks about the listing. The front door answers from your blind teaser and nothing else. A question it cannot answer comes to you.

  2. 2

    Qualify

    It applies your rules for that listing: a signed NDA, a buyer profile, proof of funds. A buyer who stops here never reaches your inbox.

  3. 3

    Introduce

    You get a short summary of the buyer and a call at one of the times you offered. You take it from there.

What you control

  • Nothing confidential is released before your conditions are met.
  • You set the rules for each listing.
  • You see a log of every exchange.

Blind price check

A price check where nobody shows their number

The buyer privately enters the most they would pay. You privately enter the least your seller would take. Both of you get one answer: a deal is possible, or there is no overlap.

A person cannot do this job. Once someone has heard your walk-away price, they cannot unsee it.

A neutral service can compare two numbers, answer yes or no, and discard both. You skip weeks of talks with a buyer who was never in range.

For buyers

For buyers and their agents

One address per listing

Each listing gets one address. A buyer can open it in a browser to read the facts, ask questions and request access. A buyer's AI agent can use the same address directly, at any hour, and get the same answers.

One profile, reused

A verified buyer profile carries identity, proof of funds, and what the buyer's agent is allowed to agree to. The buyer sets it up once and reuses it across listings.

For technical readers

Each front door publishes a machine-readable description of what it can answer and what it requires. Agents reach it over HTTPS. The buyer profile is a signed credential the front door can verify. The price check is a separate service that both sides call. Write to us for the developer notes.

Questions

What brokers ask first

Does it replace me?
No. It handles the first questions and the paperwork check. Judging the buyer, managing the seller and making the deal stay with you.
What about confidentiality?
It works from the blind teaser until your conditions are met. The business name, the address and the financials stay with you until then, and you decide what is released after.
How private is the price check?
The service compares the two numbers, reports yes or no to both sides, then discards them. The log keeps no numbers. Today this rests on trusting a neutral operator, not on cryptography.
Do buyers need an AI agent?
No. A buyer can use the front door in a browser. Agents are welcome when they arrive.
What does it cost?
Nothing during the pilot. Pricing comes after we learn what it is worth to you.
